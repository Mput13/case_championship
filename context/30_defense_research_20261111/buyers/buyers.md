# Buyers research — OMS (11 Nov 2026)

## Scope and selection

Source network is the pinned step-1 dataset at `0b0fbcb742ea2e54e05b80a5b8e890200fd5daae`; base OMS has OMS–MOW, OMS–IKT, OMS–UUD, OMS–GOJ and OMS–KUF among its modeled pairs. This appendix examines those five only. It does not add destinations or alter the OMS strategy. The pinned dashboard cards and cross-role existing buyer case files were not present in the supplied local pinned-package snapshot, so no card is treated as an existing signed program.

## Strongest observations

1. **OMS–UUD, 2026 medical travel procurement.** A tender listing (0302100024826000396) explicitly names both Omsk→Ulan-Ude and the reverse for airline tickets to rehabilitation/sanatorium treatment. This is the best exact-OD signal. The listing states one service unit per route line, but no passenger count, seats, fare, awardee, schedule, or recurrence is verified. It is a one-off public-sector ticketing need, not a committed Red Wings block or a regular corporate rotation. It can only support a lead for buyer/agent qualification; no trips are quantified.

2. **Omsk staff travel contract, route unknown.** Procurement 32616272735 reports a 499,000 RUB contract for staff travel tickets, with both air and rail categories and no usable route allocation. The total cannot be divided by an assumed fare, nor attributed to OMS–MOW or another network pair. It demonstrates institutional purchasing, not demand for a modeled OD.

3. **Shift transport is bus-only.** A 2027–2028 procurement for the Omsk air navigation center specifies bus transport for shift staff on 365/366 daily units. This is future, local ground transport; it does not establish aviation demand or a candidate route. Excluded from the route opportunity count.

## Search record and limits

One main web pass used eight narrow queries: procurement notices by OMS–IKT/UUD/GOJ/KUF/MOW; employer shift/rotation travel for OMS–IKT/UUD; corporate/group travel on OMS–GOJ/KUF; and Omsk-origin group tour programs. Public search surfaced exact UUD medical travel, an unallocated mixed travel contract, and bus-only staff movement. It did not surface route-specific contracts, protocols, recurring shift rotations, or actual packaged airline programs for the other selected pairs. Search absence is not evidence that demand is absent. No second pass was warranted: no contradictory primary evidence arose. Exact EIS originals/attachments were not validated in this pass; the tender aggregation pages are provisional evidence.

## Facts, assumptions, unknowns

- **Fact:** the pinned network contains the listed base OMS pairs; the tender listing names OMS–UUD ticketing both directions; a separate Omsk staff travel purchase is mixed mode and unallocated.
- **Assumption:** none used to infer buyer volume or convert contract totals to passengers.
- **UNKNOWN:** ultimate legal buyer/beneficiary and award status for the OMS–UUD listing; passenger counts; actual tickets flown; recurrence; tender attachments; direct/connecting itinerary; exact product match to a proposed RW schedule; route shares of the Omsk staff travel contract; all route-specific buyer volumes for OMS–IKT/GOJ/KUF/MOW.
