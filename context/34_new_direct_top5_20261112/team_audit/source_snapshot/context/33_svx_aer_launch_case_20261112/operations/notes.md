The 210-minute blocks reuse RW’s undated 3h30 route-page average as a scenario in each direction; reverse block, type/variant-specific dispatch and actual tail remain UNKNOWN and require operational verification.

KEEP is the no-new-AER baseline with the donor assignment represented as the removed candidate rotation; retained sectors match P. There are no SVX→SVX placeholders. All P32 times were timezone-checked: SVX UTC+5, KUF UTC+4, OMS UTC+6; dates can shift in UTC. Removing OMS is a proposed donor choice, not evidence it must be removed.
