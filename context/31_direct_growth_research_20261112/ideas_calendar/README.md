## Best testable mechanisms

1. **Shift SVX–GOJ weekday/time:** current public listing shows Mon/Fri pattern through 26 March 2027 and dated Nov flights at a different midday time. This is a plausible calendar experiment, not evidence the days are weak/strong. Test day-specific bookings/contribution and slot/crew feasibility against the current pattern; require delta contribution over donor assignment > 0.
2. **Retiming existing direct service:** move a rotation into an evidenced local departure-time gap while preserving aircraft hours. Measure directed booking curves and net contribution before/after, accounting for missed connections and opportunity cost. Current evidence is insufficient to say which time is better.
3. **Season split:** choose seasonal concentration only after monthly RW OD history and dated competitor calendars. Do not project winter 2026 listings into H2 2027.
4. **Replace frequency rather than add a pair:** reallocate one rotation from a weaker day/route to an existing or new direct opportunity; compare whole-network delta versus KEEP, including displaced route results.
5. **Directional frequency asymmetry:** evaluate each direction and weekday independently from actual bookings/yield, not fare or published-frequency symmetry. Current directional LF is UNKNOWN.
