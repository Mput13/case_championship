# Newsreel — 2024

In 2024, Aeroflot continued to develop its route network, improve its service, and increase transportation accessibility for Russia's regions.

Aeroflot exceeded its passenger traffic forecasts for 2024 – the Group's airlines carried 55.3 million passengers, up 16.8% from 2023 (47.3 million). Domestic routes carried 42.6 million passengers, 12.8% more than in 2023. International flights also showed significant growth – 32.7% or 12.7 million passengers.

In January and February, Aeroflot finalized settlements with two groups of lessors for seven more aircraft and in December, Aeroflot was the first airline in Russia to start flying under a 'wet' leasing program. Under the terms of the agreement, iFly Airlines transferred three Airbus A330-300 wide-body airliners along with their flight and cabin crews to Aeroflot. At the first stage of the schedule integration, Aeroflot will use these aircraft on flights from Moscow to cities in the Far East.
