About Us
JSC «Red Wings» was established in 1999 and was originally known as Airlines 400. It got its current name in 2007. Today, Red Wings is one of the ten largest Russian airlines, specializing in scheduled and charter passenger transportation.
The itinerary network of Red Wings consists of more than 100 Russian and international destinations.
The airline's fleet consists of 24 passenger aircraft: 14 Airbus 320/321, seven Russian regional SSJ100s and three long-range Boeing 777s. Base airports are Domodedovo, Koltsovo (Yekaterinburg) and Zhukovsky.
Within the period of April, 5 to 9 JSC «Red Wings» underwent initial IATA safety audit (IOSA) to achieve the safety compliance IATA certificate. By the result there being prepared a corrective actions report.
The operational quality control in JSC «Red Wings» is being fulfilled in accordance with the approved Quality assurance program that complies with national and international quality requirements.
