Flight Opeartion Centralized Unified System
The UAE Aviation Information Portal for Air Operators
Welcome to your official gateway to the UAE's aviation sector. This platform is here to provide you with all the resources you need to align seamlessly with UAE's regulatory and operational requirements.
Explore tools, access critical information, and stay informed with updates tailored to support your operations in the UAE's dynamic aviation environment.
One Portal
All UAE permits and aviation services centralized for your convenience.
Connect & Explore
Access the latest regulatory requirements, forms, circulars, and guidance.
Operate with Ease
Stay updated with real-time alerts and insights tailored for air operators.
Scheduled Services
Embark on a seamless journey—get designated, get registered, and get your seasonal permits in a few clicks!
1. Confirm Your Designation Rights
If not yet designated, apply through your State Civil Aviation Authority.
Once designated, return to complete the following steps.
2. Register as a Foreign Air Operator
On the
GCAA Website
register as an
Individual User
- Create a
Corporate Account
by selecting Organization Type "Foreign Air Operator" under the Individual profile.
Submit your application for
Foreign Operator Registration (FOR GCAP).
3. Register with the National Advance Information Center (NAIC)
You cannot obtain any seasonal or landing permits without your NAIC Certificate of Completion. -
Register Here
Support:
Inside UAE: 800274823
Outside UAE: +971 800 274 823
Email:
API@icp.gov.ae
4. Apply for Seasonal Permits through the Appropriate Authority of the Emirate:
Abu Dhabi & Al Ain:
ADM
Dubai:
DCAA
Sharjah:
DCA Sharjah
Ras Al Khaimah: Contact Mr. Saeed Al Yammahi, Manager Air Transport and International Affairs Section, Department of Civil Aviation at E-mail:
saeed.r@dca.rak.ae
Fujairah: Contact Mr. Esmaeil Boloushi, General Manager, Fujairah Airport at E-mail:
emh@fia.ae
Need Help?
For GCAA Foreign Operator Registration Acceptance (FOR GCAP) support, contact:
gcap@gcaa.gov.ae
For NAIC Support, contact the API-UAE call center or above Email
For Permit-specific queries, reach out directly to the appropriate authority of the Emirate.
For support related to Air Operator Certification or Authorization in the UAE (for UAE Operators), please contact:
fops@gcaa.gov.ae
Non-Scheduled Services
Follow these three steps to secure non-scheduled, charter landing permits across the UAE.
1. Register as a Foreign Operator
Log in to the Foreign Operator Registration GCAA Acceptance (FOR GCAP) e-Service platform and complete your application.
2. Register with the National Advance Information Center (NAIC)
A NAIC Certificate of Completion is mandatory before any landing permit is issued. -
Register Here
Support:
Inside UAE: 800274823
Outside UAE: +971 800 274 823
Email:
API@icp.gov.ae
3. Apply for Ad-hoc Landing Permit
Choose the appropriate authority based on the Emirate where you wish to operate:
