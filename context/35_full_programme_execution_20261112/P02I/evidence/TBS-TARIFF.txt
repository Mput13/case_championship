                                    AIRPORT CHARGES AT TBILISI AIRPORT


This AIC replaces AIC A 17/25 dated 13 NOV 2025.


Information effective from 01 JAN 2026.

1    Landing and take-off charges
The amount of the landing and take-off charges that are payable to TAV Urban Georgia LLC is calculated as follows:
– for aircraft with MTOW up to 14 tons – at the rate of 2.5 USD per 1 ton;
– for aircraft with MTOW more than 14 tons – at the rate of 7.5 USD per 1 ton (see table 1 for more details).
Table 1.

               Flight Category                  Aircraft MTOW till 14 tons (included)           Aircraft MTOW over 14 tons
                 International                              2.5 USD / ton                               7.5 USD / ton
                   Domestic                                 2.5 USD / ton                                5 USD / ton


In case of take-offs and landings which proceed between 20.00 and 06.00 local time during the summer season (from 01.04
to 31.10) and between 18.00 and 08.00 local time during the winter season (from 01.11 to 31.03), landing charge will be
increased by 20% for the utilization of lighting facilities.

2    Aircraft Parking charges

2.1 Principles of Application
a) Parking Charges are applicable according to the MTOW of the aircraft. Fractions of a ton are to be rounded off upwards.
b) For the flights of foreign air carriers, the first 3 hours from arrival for regular and charter flights and the first 6 hours from
   arrival for cargo flights are free of charge. Parking over 3 hours for scheduled flights are calculated on per hour basis in
   accordance with Table 2. Parking over 3 hours for charters and over 6 hours for cargo flights are calculated as overnight
   parking (see Table 3 for more details).
c) The duration for parking begins from the landing of an aircraft and ends with the start-up clearance, which is given by
   the tower.

2.3 Parking charges for local based aircraft
a) Local based cargo aircraft are exempted from charges for the period of 14 days upon the first date of arrival in Tbilisi
   Airport.
b) Local based passenger aircraft are exempted from charges for the period of 30 days upon the first date of arrival in
   Tbilisi Airport.
c) After completion of the above mentioned period the parking fee per each day and night (every 24 hours) shall be as set
   forth below:
   Table 3a – Parking Charges

              MTOW (tons) (For local based aircraft)                                       Charge (USD)
                            1 – 50 tons                                                       100 USD
                           51 – 100 tons                                                      200 USD
                           101 – 200 tons                                                     275 USD
                           over 201 tons                                                      375 USD


d) Calculation of an aircraft parking time for cargo aircraft shall commence after expiration of a 14 days period from the
   moment of landing of an aircraft in Tbilisi, as recorded in the service documents by Ramp Tower.
e) Calculation of an aircraft parking time for passenger aircraft shall commence after expiration of a 30 days period from
   the moment of landing of an aircraft in Tbilisi, as recorded in the service documents by Ramp Tower.
f) For the purpose of calculating the parking fees each incomplete day and night period shall be rounded to 24 hours.

7     Exemptions and reductions

7.1 Exemptions and reductions granted by TAV Urban Georgia LLC
a) Flights are exempted from the take-off and landing fee in case they are operating for emergency or rescue works for
   Georgia.
b) Aircraft are exempted from the Parking Charges in the cases of:
   – bad meteorological conditions at the airport of destination or departure;
   – emergency landings;
   – extra parking due to aircraft technical problems.
c) Passenger aircraft of a local based air carrier at Tbilisi/Tbilisi Airport are exempted from parking charges for the period
   of first 30 days parking after each landing. Cargo aircraft of local based air carrier at Tbilisi/Tbilisi Airport are exempted
   from parking charges for the period of first 14 days parking after each landing.
d) Aircraft are exempted from the Safety Precautions Charges in the cases of:
   – aircraft on search and rescue;
   – aircraft carrying aid material, aid personnel and their accompanies;
   – services given for bomb warning.
e) Aircraft with MTOW up to 14 tons (including) are exempted from the take-off and landing fee and the fee shall be
   decreased by 50% for the aircraft with maximum take- off weight above 14 tons, in the cases of:
   – flights landing at TBILISI/Tbilisi Airport as an alternate airport, due to bad meteorological conditions at the airport of
        destination;
   – emergency landings (due to aircrafts technical disorder or in the event of presence of a sick person on board);
   – demonstration flights not exceeding 30 minutes, provided notice given beforehand (demonstration flights also
        include the test or training flights).
7.2 Passenger service charge is not paid in the cases of:
   – children at 2 years of age;
   – flight having heads of states;
   – crew members taking place on flights crew list and holding ID cards;
   – flight carrying aid materials and/or aid personnel;
   – flight landing because of technical reasons;
   – flight returning to the departure airport due to technical reasons;
   – flight diverted due to any reason avoiding to land to the origin;
   – flight assigned to the military or civilian staff, who will perform official visits, and those notified by the related ministry.
7.3 Exemptions and reductions granted by Security
Passenger Security Service Fees are not applicable under conditions mentioned below:
   – minors 2 years old and under;
   – passengers of humanitarian flights
   – passengers that have not left the aircraft (direct transit);
   – for sky Marshalls (if they are returning with the same flight).

50% discount for the cost of the aviation security service charges is made under the following circumstances:
   – if aerodrome is used as an alternate aerodrome, while it is impossible/inadvisable to land at the aerodrome of
      intended landing because of the bad weather conditions;
   – emergency landing;
   – demonstration flight;
   – in case of search and rescue operations;
   – if accident has happened during landing;
   – emergency landing, where is it caused by an act of unlawful interference in civil aviation.
A fifteen percent (15%) surcharge for services provided for aircraft is applicable for the flights arriving or departing in the
following periods: from 22:00 till 06:00 local time.
In calculating the ACFT MTOW each portion of a ton will be calculated as full ton.
The price does not include taxes envisaged by Georgian Legislation.
Flights, which serve to a significant public interest and/or are aimed to eradicate the conditions which threaten human lives,
health and/or safety, and/or a possibility of such conditions to occur, could be exempted from charges envisaged by this
price list. Such conditions include, but are not limited to, earthquake, flood, heavy snowfall, fire, landslide, storm, state of
emergency, war, martial actions and/or any other force majeure conditions, which are either publicly acknowledged and/or
confirmed by the respectively competent person/authority.

8   Methods of payment
Passenger service charge is included in passenger ticket fare. The money collected by the air carriers are paid to TAV
Urban Georgia LLC in terms of the contracts signed mutually or on cash basis, if there is no contract signed.
The charges can be collected by the airlines officers during check-in, in case of necessity.
