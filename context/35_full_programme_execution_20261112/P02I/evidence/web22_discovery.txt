L2: ### GEN 1.2  ENTRY, TRANSIT AND DEPARTURE OF AIRCRAFT
L3: #### 1.   GENERAL
L4: 
L5: International flights into, from or over the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan shall be subject to the current regulations and legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan relating to civil aviation, as listed in 【0†GEN-1.6】.
L6: 
L7: These regulations are complemented by Standards and Recommended Practices contained in Annex 9 to the Convention on International Civil Aviation, with the differences given in 【1†GEN-1.7】.
L8: Aircraft flying into or departing from the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan shall make its first landing at, or final departure from, an international aerodrome (see 【2†AD-1.3】), where all necessary conditions for customs and immigration clearance are available.
L9: National airports of the Republic of Kazakhstan (see 【2†AD-1.3】) and aerodromes/ heliports not indicated as international or designated for receiving aircraft from CIS countries only, do not provide ATS, MET and ground services in English. Therefore, international flights with landings at these airports (aerodromes/heliports) shall be made only in association with a person having a valid pilot or navigator certificate issued by the Civil Aviation Committee of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
L10: Aerodromes approved for international flights and published in AIS documents may be used as alternate aerodromes.
L11: #### 2.   SCHEDULED FLIGHTS
L12: ##### 2.1   General
L13: 
L14: For regular international scheduled flights operated by foreign airlines into or in transit across the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan, the following requirements must be met:
L15:   1. the State of the airline must be a party to the International Air Services Transit Agreement and/or the International Air Transport Agreement. The Republic of Kazakhstan is a party to both Agreements;
L16:   2. the airline must be eligible to make the flights under the provisions of a bilateral or multilateral agreement to which the State of the airline and the Republic of Kazakhstan are contracting parties and must have a permit to operate into or in transit across the territory of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
L17: All regular flights in the airspace of the Republic of Kazakhstan shall be made in accordance with flight schedules, which must be submitted in 4 (four) copies of the form "R" to the Committee of Civil Aviation of the Republic of Kazakhstan (CCA RK) and the Main Center for Air Traffic Flow Management (MC ATFM) no later than 30 (thirty) days prior to the flight and to be approved by CCA RK.
L18: Any changes to the submitted regular flight schedule shall be sent in English or Russian to the MC ATFM, no later than 10 (ten) days prior to flight operation, via following communication channels:

L27: ##### 2.2   Documentary requirements for clearance of aircraft
L28: 
L29: All documents listed below must follow the ICAO standard format as set forth in the relevant appendices to ICAO Annex 9 and are acceptable when furnished in English or Russian.
L30: 
L31: Foreign aircraft operators must submit aircraft documents for clearance on entry and departure to/from the Republic of Kazakhstan.
L32: List of documentation to obtain a permission for regular flights with a stopover (arrival/departure) in airports of the Republic of Kazakhstan:
L33:   1. Air operator certificate (for foreign, CIS and national operators);
L34:   2. Annex to the operator's certificate (for CIS and national operators);
L35:   3. Aircraft registration certificate (for foreign, CIS and national operators);
L36:   4. Aircraft airworthiness certificate (for foreign, CIS and national operators);
L37:   5. Civil liability insurance to third parties and passengers;
L38:   6. Aircraft insurance certificate (for CIS and national operators);
L39:   7. Crew insurance licence (for CIS and national operators);
L40:   8. Form “R” signed by the head of the airline;
L41:   9. Repetitive flight plan (RPL) according to ICAO Doc. 4444;
L42:   10. Slot times coordinated with destinations of the Republic of Kazakhstan.
L43: List of documentation to obtain a permission for regular transit flights:
L44:   1. Airline operator licence (for all foreign operators);
L45:   2. Annex to the operator's certificate (for operators of CIS countries only);
L46:   3. Certificate of aircraft’s registration (for operators of CIS countries only);
L47:   4. Airworthiness certificate (for operators of CIS countries only);
L48:   5. Civil liability insurance to third parties and passengers (for all foreign operators and CIS countries);
L49:   6. Repetitive flight plan (RPL) according to ICAO Doc 4444.
L50: Permission for international non-scheduled flights is issued free of charge

L57: 【22†办事条件:】
L58:     外国航空运输企业（以下简称“外航”）申请经营外国地点和中华人民共和国地点间规定航线，应当符合中外双方政府民用航空运输协定或者有关协议的规定，并先经其本国政府通过外交途径对其进行指定，双方航空运输协定或有关协议另有规定的除外。
L59: 【23†办事程序:】
L60:     1、外国航空运输企业应当在计划开航之日60日前向民航局提交经营航线的申请书及相关材料。
L61:     2、申请材料齐全、符合法定形式的，民航局受理外航的申请。申请材料不齐全或者不符合法定形式的，民航局在五个工作日内一次性通知该外航需要补充的全部内容，逾期不通知的，自收到申请材料之日起即为受理。
L62: 外航按要求补齐全部材料后，民航局受理申请，申请材料经补正后仍不符合要求的，民航局不予受理，并出具不予受理的书面凭证。
L63:     3、除双方航空运输协定或有关协议另有规定外，民航局自受理申请之日起二十个工作日内做出是否批准的决定。民航局在二十个工作日内不能做出决定的，经民航局局长批准，可以延长十个工作日，并将延长期限的理由告知外航.
L64:     4、民航局依法做出批准决定后，自做出批准决定之日起十个工作日内向外航颁发经营许可。民航局依法做出不予批准决定的，向外航出具书面决定并说明理由。
L65:     5、外国航空运输企业获得航线经营许可后，应当按航季向民航局申报国际航班计划，获得批准后，方可执行。（外国航空运输企业开航前应办理的其他手续、相关规定及相应部门联系方式见《外航申请开通定期航线程序指南（中、英文版）》【24†http://sqgk.caac.gov.cn/000014170/201507/t20150703_12082.htm†sqgk.caac.gov.cn】 ）
L66:     6、外国航空运输企业以代号共享或湿租方式经营国际航班的，应当向民航局提交申请及相关材料，获得批准后，方可执行。
L67: 材料：
L68:     外航申请经营许可应当向民航局递交由该外航总部法定代表人或者经其书面授权的人员使用中文或者英文签发的申请书及其附带材料。
L69:     （一）申请书应当包括以下内容：
L70:     计划开通的外国地点和中华人民共和国地点间的规定航线、开航日期、航班号和代码共享航班号、每周班次和班期、本企业所有或者以湿租方式租赁的飞机机型和航空器登记号。
L71:     （二）外航随申请书一并提交的附带材料包括：
L72:     （1）外国政府指定该外航经营外国地点和中华人民共和国地点间规定航线的文件复印件；
L73:     （2）外国政府航空主管部门为该外航颁发的从事公共航空运输的航空经营人许可证（AOC）复印件；
L74:     （3）企业注册证明复印件；
L75:     （4）企业章程或由法定企业登记机构出具的，载有企业主要营业地、企业性质（国有或者私有）、股份结构、投资方国籍及董事会成员姓名和国籍的证明文件；

L87:     1、外航申请经营许可应当在计划开航之日六十日前向民航局提出。

L90:     3、除双方航空运输协定或有关协议另有规定外，民航局自受理申请之日起二十个工作日内做出是否批准的决定。民航局在二十个工作日内不能做出决定的，经民航局局长批准，可以延长十个工作日，并将延长期限的理由告知外航。
L91:     4、民航局依法做出批准决定后，自做出批准决定之日起十个工作日内向外航颁发经营许可。民航局依法做出不予批准决定的，向外航出具书面决定并说明理由。
