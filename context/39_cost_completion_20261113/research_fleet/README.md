# Research fleet prices — 2026-11-13

1. **Only reusable numeric benchmark is a narrow, old S7 Technics RRJ-95 tariff.** Its 2023 dated USD rates cover discrete line tasks/secondment and parts storage, not an OMS maintenance package. No 2027 FX or escalation rule was evidenced, so the historical rates remain unconverted and non-admitted.
2. **RW insurance premium remains UNKNOWN.** RW’s 2025 primary announcement confirms one-year whole-fleet cover and >RUB 78bn insured asset value, but gives no premium. Do not turn insured value into annual cost.
3. **RW lease payment remains UNKNOWN.** The company confirms two SSJ100 were leased in 2022, without payment schedule; the 2023 Rostec SJ-100 deal likewise discloses no price. Capital value/deposit is not annual rent.
4. **SaM146 reserve/exchange, OMS AOG logistics, tools and actual warehouse need are unpriced.** The S7 price list's travel exclusion is not a quote. Distinguish reserve expense per engine FH from exchange-pool deposits/working capital.
5. **Additional crew, hotel/per diem/positioning/training, and launch/distribution costs remain UNKNOWN.** Request RW roster and vendor invoices. Cancellation of a flight does not necessarily save fixed rent or already-employed salary. Donor flight contribution is an internal counterfactual, not a cash charge.

`price_evidence.csv` contains component-level evidence, units, source/effective dates, tax/cost basis, confidence, overlap gate, calculation need and exact RW request. No annual network total is calculated. Ground/OMS office, baggage, passenger hotels, non-technical station staffing, tariff prices and fuel are outside this file.
