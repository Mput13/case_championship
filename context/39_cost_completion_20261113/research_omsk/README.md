# OMS base cost research — 2026-11-13

Scope: lease/infrastructure, GSE/IT/DCS/comms, local station staff, incremental transfer baggage, and passenger hotel/meal/transfer expense after disrupted connections. Maintenance, parts, and airframe lease excluded.

Read alongside P30 `base_costs`, `review_costs`, `review_evidence`, and P38 `cost_admission`. Those packages already warn that the OMS tariff disclosure has not yielded an admitted price, the model's ground basket (96,089/115,307 RUB per flight) is not itemized, and there were zero new quantitative model admissions. P38 SVX tariffs remain context; no duplication or automatic price updates were made here.

## Findings

- OMS's official tariff page opened and lists its Russian customer rate card from 21 Aug 2026 plus Oct amendments. The linked generic handling agreement says charges follow effective published/airport-approved rates. The accessible evidence does not establish a dedicated base rent, office/warehouse/apron lease, communications/DCS fee, or OMS discount. Request itemized prices and terms; no price invented.
- Only the primary Kamchatka Airport 2025 tariff provides a quantitative incremental bag-transfer analogue: 16.90 RUB/kg, ex VAT, by airline request. This is not OMS, does not demonstrate whether sorting/through-baggage is included, and may not be added when identical handling is already purchased. Marked as analogue, not a Red Wings price.
- SVX primary transfer rules identify hotel, meals, and transfers as disruption cost categories and allocate reimbursement to the airline responsible for delay/cancellation. They provide no unit rates and no incidence. Both remain UNKNOWN; do not calculate expected annual cost without exposure and event count.
- A secondary aggregator search result reports 42,000–50,000 RUB for an Omsk Airport passenger service agent vacancy dated 10 Sep 2026. It is only role/salary context, not primary-verified and not a Red Wings quote. It establishes no FTE count or shift roster.
- GSE/tools/DCS/IT/channel costs have no admitted evidence. Buying equipment and outsourcing the same function are mutually exclusive cases. Avoid double counting ordinary handling.

No annualization or total budget calculation was made. UNKNOWN is not zero. `price_evidence.csv` leaves unavailable rates and quantities explicitly UNKNOWN and includes exact RW follow-up requests. See `actual_queries.json` and `excerpts/` for the bounded web pass and source passages.
