# Operations and cost research

Scope is existing KEEP assignments, aircraft, airport/turn constraints, overnight positioning, fuel and avoidability. Sources are P34/P08/P09 frozen snapshots plus a bounded official-source web pass.

## Findings

- Official Red Wings winter schedule announcement states the season runs through 27 March 2027 and covers 71 destinations (34 international). It is a network statement, not a tail/crew assignment. Individual tail and crew at SVX/ZIA/DME remain UNKNOWN.
- Carrier fleet page reports 20 SuperJet-100 and 3 Boeing 777 aircraft; this does not establish free/dispatchable aircraft. Mixed SSJ/Tu patterns, ZIA bus, or B777 DME-CXR cannot be used as evidence of a free RRJ tail.
- P08-SVX dated rotations are illustrative templates; its own handoff says full KEEP resource and operational permissions remain unknown. Use actual directional route block data where present; otherwise UNKNOWN. Turn 45/60/90 minutes are diagnostics absent RW contracts.
- Ufa's official tariff PDF is an airport-specific public tariff, not an RW network contract. The queried 133200/136300 + 1696 fuel values were not verified as a compatible row/tax basis here. Do not transfer them to SVX/AER/MOW. Net/gross and VAT treatment remain UNKNOWN pending exact primary table row/invoice.
- Public terminal hours do not confer a free slot. RRJ arrival does not prove contract MRO. 95B permission is not PD8 support. Passport range or universal +20% is not payload dispatch.
- Fuel burn, uplift/landing/handling/cycle costs may be marginally avoidable; negotiated amounts are UNKNOWN. Lease and fixed crew are not automatically avoidable. Overnight parking, hotel, transfer, station, reserve and actual maintenance windows remain UNKNOWN. 2027 grants/credits are not guaranteed.
- SJ-100 is a post-H2 2027 scenario only after scenario delivery and certification. PD8 support is not RRJ support.
